Privacy Policy

Application: JARVIS E-Commerce

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

Overview

JARVIS E-Commerce is a personal automation platform for marketplace research, product analysis, profitability calculations, and workflow automation.

Information We Access

  • Marketplace listing information
  • Inventory information
  • Product catalog information
  • Order information (when authorized)
  • Pricing information

How Information Is Used

  • Analyze product opportunities
  • Calculate ROI and profitability
  • Support marketplace automation
  • Generate reports and recommendations

Data Sharing

No personal information is sold or rented. Data accessed through authorized marketplace APIs is used only to operate the application.

Credential Security

OAuth credentials are stored securely and used only for authenticated API access.

User Control

Users may revoke access through their marketplace account or by removing stored credentials.
